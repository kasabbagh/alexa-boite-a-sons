# Privacy Policy - Boîte à Sons

**Last updated: June 12, 2026**

## Overview

Boîte à Sons is an Alexa skill that plays sound effects (animals, vehicles, nature, etc.). This skill is designed to be simple and fun.

## Data Collection

This skill does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not:

- Collect your name, email, or contact information
- Store voice recordings
- Track your usage or location
- Share any data with third parties
- Use cookies or analytics

## Children's Privacy

This skill is safe for use by children. No personal data is collected from any user, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with the new date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue on this repository.
